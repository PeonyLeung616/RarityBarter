The Wet Paper Towel Cutting Board Stabilizer

Quick Summary
Use a slightly damp paper towel or thin kitchen cloth underneath a cutting board to reduce movement on a smooth countertop.

Why It Works
A cutting board can slide when chopping because the surfaces underneath it have relatively little friction. A thin damp layer between the board and countertop can increase friction and help the board remain more stable.

Step-by-Step
1. Place the cutting board on a clean countertop.
2. Take one sheet of paper towel or a thin kitchen cloth.
3. Dampen it with water.
4. Wring it out so that it is damp rather than dripping wet.
5. Lay it flat underneath the cutting board.
6. Place the cutting board on top.
7. Press down gently and check that the board does not move easily before starting to cut.

Best For
- Wooden cutting boards
- Plastic cutting boards
- Smooth kitchen countertops
- Marble, granite, laminate, and similar surfaces

Important Safety Note
The damp towel reduces movement but does not make a cutting board completely immovable. Always keep one hand safely away from the knife blade and use normal knife-handling techniques.

Maintenance
If the towel becomes soaked or starts moving underneath the board, replace it or adjust it. Wash and dry reusable kitchen cloths after use.

Why It Is Useful
It is a cheap and readily available solution when a cutting board does not have rubber feet or a built-in non-slip base.
