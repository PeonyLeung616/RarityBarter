The Ice Cube Dryer Steam De-Wrinkler

Quick Summary
Smooth out lightly wrinkled clothing when you do not have convenient access to an iron or clothes steamer.

How It Works
Place two or three ordinary ice cubes into the tumble dryer together with the wrinkled clothing. Select a short, warm-to-high heat drying cycle appropriate for the garments.

As the dryer heats up, the ice melts and the resulting moisture becomes warm water vapour. The humid environment can help relax some fabric fibres and reduce light wrinkles.

Step-by-Step
1. Check the clothing care label first and make sure the garment is suitable for tumble drying.
2. Put the wrinkled garments into the dryer.
3. Add two or three ice cubes.
4. Run a short drying cycle using a temperature appropriate for the clothing.
5. When the cycle finishes, remove the clothes promptly.
6. Shake the garments gently and hang them up while they are still warm.

Best For
- Lightly wrinkled cotton clothing
- Cotton-blend shirts
- Everyday trousers
- Clothes that only need a quick refresh

Limitations
This method is not a replacement for ironing or professional steaming. Very deep creases may remain, and delicate fabrics may require different treatment.

Safety Note
Always follow the garment's care label. Do not use high heat on clothing that is not rated for it. Avoid using this method with delicate fabrics, garments that require dry cleaning, or materials that can be damaged by heat or moisture.

Why It Is Useful
The trick is useful when travelling, when an iron is unavailable, or when only a few lightly wrinkled garments need a quick refresh.
